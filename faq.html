FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before your visit.

How do I book an appointment?

Use our online booking form, WhatsApp us, or call the clinic. Our team will confirm the requested slot.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring any relevant dental records, previous X-rays if available, and a list of medications or allergies.

How long does a dental appointment take?

It depends on the service. A first consultation generally takes around 30–45 minutes.

Do you offer emergency appointments?

We try to accommodate urgent dental concerns. Call the clinic directly for the fastest response.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes. Contact the clinic as early as possible and our team will help find another suitable time.
